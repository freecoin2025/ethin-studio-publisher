What is Ethin Studio Publisher?

Ethin Studio Publisher is a web application that helps video creators publish content to TikTok through the official TikTok Content Posting API. Instead of manually transferring finished videos to a phone, creators authorize once with their TikTok account and upload drafts or publish directly from the desktop studio.

How it works

  1. Connect — Sign in with your TikTok account via secure OAuth 2.0 authorization.
  2. Upload — Send your finished video file to TikTok as a draft, or publish it directly.
  3. Done — Your video appears on your TikTok profile, ready for your audience.

Key features

TikTok OAuth login Draft upload Direct publishing Desktop workflow

Who is it for?

Ethin Studio Publisher is built for creators and small studios that produce TikTok content on desktop editing tools and want a reliable, official way to get finished videos onto TikTok without manual file transfers.

Data & privacy

We take your data seriously. Ethin Studio Publisher only requests the minimum TikTok permissions needed to upload and publish videos. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details.
