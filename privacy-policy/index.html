Ethin Studio Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

Ethin Studio Publisher ("we", "our", "the app") is a web application that helps creators publish videos to TikTok via the official TikTok Content Posting API. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

2. Data we collect

  • TikTok account data: when you authorize the app via TikTok OAuth 2.0, we receive an access token, refresh token, your TikTok open_id, and basic profile information (such as display name) needed to upload and publish videos on your behalf.
  • Video files: video files you choose to upload through the app are transmitted to TikTok's servers for draft creation or direct publishing. We do not retain copies of your videos after the upload completes.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) for security and debugging purposes.

3. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you with TikTok and perform the uploads you request.
  • To maintain your session (via stored OAuth tokens) so you do not need to re-authorize on every visit.
  • To diagnose errors and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except TikTok itself, as required to perform the publishing actions you request.

4. Data storage & security

OAuth tokens are stored securely and are never exposed in client-side code or public pages. Access to production credentials is restricted.

5. Your rights

You may revoke the app's access to your TikTok account at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). You may also contact us to request deletion of any data we hold about you.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent changes.

7. Contact

For privacy questions regarding Ethin Studio Publisher, please contact us through the TikTok Developer Portal support channel linked in the app's developer profile.
